Card condition guide

Every card we price is graded by hand. Here's what each condition means.

NM
Near MintLooks fresh from the pack. At most a tiny speck or edge nick you'd have to look for.
LP
Lightly PlayedLight wear: a few small edge or corner marks or light scratches. No creases.
MP
Moderately PlayedWear you can see right away: worn edges and corners, scratches, maybe a small crease.
HP
Heavily PlayedHeavy wear all over, with creases or bends, but still in one piece.
DMG
DamagedTears, big creases, water damage, writing or missing pieces.

How our prices work

Our prices start from each card's TCGplayer market price, updated every night, and are rounded by our own price rules.

Played cards use TCGplayer's market price for that condition when there is one. When there isn't, our staff price the card by hand, and some show "Ask staff" until they do.

The QR sticker on every card opens its price for today, in every condition. Scan it with your phone's camera or at our table.
