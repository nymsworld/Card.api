Card condition guide

What each condition looks like, front and back. Tap a card or drag it sideways to turn it over.

Near Mint (NM)

Simple definition

A Near Mint card shows minimal wear from shuffling, play, or handling.

Key details
  • Minimal wear; may have a nearly unmarked surface
  • Crisp corners and unblemished edges outside of a few slight flaws
  • May have slight edge wear or a couple minor scratches
  • Overall look nearly unplayed with no major defects
  • Manufacturing defects (e.g., miscuts/shifting) may result in the card being considered Damaged

Lightly Played (LP)

Simple definition

A Lightly Played card may have minor edge wear, scuffs, or scratches.

Key details
  • May have minor edge wear, scuffs, or scratches
  • No significant imperfections or issues with structural integrity
  • Noticeable imperfections are okay
  • None should be too severe or at too high a volume

Moderately Played (MP)

Simple definition

A Moderately Played card shows moderate signs of use but remains structurally sound.

Key details
  • Edge wear and surface wear may be present
  • Scratches or scuffing may be visible
  • Bends with creases may be present
  • A combination of moderate imperfections is acceptable
  • No damage that affects the card’s structural integrity

Heavily Played (HP)

Simple definition

A Heavily Played card shows a major amount of wear.

Key details
  • Major amount of wear is visible
  • Many types of moderate imperfections may be present
  • Creasing, whitening, and other faults are common
  • Defects or severe imperfections may be present
  • Must still be able to be authenticated and cannot be structurally damaged

Damaged (DMG)

Simple definition

A Damaged card shows wear or imperfections beyond the standards for other conditions.

Key details
  • May have major border wear, scratching or scuffing
  • May have bends, tears, splitting, or other damage that impacts the card’s integrity
  • May have foreign substances such as food or liquid
  • Corners may be heavily worn, frayed, or rounded
  • Surface may have deep scratches, stains, or discoloration

How our prices work

Our prices start from each card's TCGplayer market price, updated every night, and are rounded by our own price rules.

Played cards use TCGplayer's market price for that condition when there is one. When there isn't, our staff price the card by hand, and some show "Ask staff" until they do.

The QR sticker on every card opens its price for today, in every condition. Scan it with your phone's camera or at our table.
